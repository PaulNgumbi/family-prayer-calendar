## What's inside

- A short prayer and a Bible verse for every day, October 2026 to December 2027
- The Our Father, one line each month, following the Church year: feasts, saints, Holy Days and liturgical colors
- A complete Rosary you can pray bead by bead, with the mysteries for each day
- Monthly faith formation for children: the sacraments, the Mass, Mary, the Bible and more
- A prayer to learn, a psalm to memorize, a question and a family activity each month
- Links to each day's Mass readings and the New American Bible on the U.S. bishops' website (usccb.org)
- Works fully offline and asks for no permissions

## Install from the link (Android or iPhone)

- **Android (Chrome):** open the app link and tap **Install app** (or ⋮ menu → **Install app**).
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the app link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

It gets its own icon, opens full-screen, and works offline.

## Install the APK (Android)

1. On your Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer), tap the download link above.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app.
3. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the developer is unknown, tap **Install anyway**. This app isn't from the Google Play Store.
4. Open **Family Prayer** from your home screen.
